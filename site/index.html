In your first week, we promise

One setup. A whole team. Better every week.

  1. 1

    Set up once

    Tell us where to find you: your website, Instagram, Google listing and country. Allevia reads them, builds your company brain and briefs ten staff. Our Setup Desk finishes the account connections with you on one optional call.

  2. 2

    Your AI team works every day

    Meera, the chief of staff, takes every request and hands it to the right desks. The morning brief, review replies, the Monday content pack and the weekly numbers arrive without you asking.

  3. 3

    Nothing is published, filed or changed until you approve it. Each card says what it is, who proposed it and why, and what approving will do.

    Edit a draft and the team keeps your change as a rule for next time, linked to the approval it came from. The share of drafts you approve without edits sits on your scorecard, so you can watch it rise or tell us when it doesn't.

Ten staff in eight departments, and you can watch them work.

Every plan has the whole company. Each person has a desk, a job and the tools for it, and the office shows who is working on what as it happens.

Every answer shows its path.

Your business lives in a company brain: a knowledge graph of your pages, products, people, numbers and decisions, built on FalkorDB. Every answer is a walk through it, and you can see the walk.

  1. Receipts on every answer

    Every claim cites a node in your company brain, and the path lights up. Citations and numbers are checked against what the team actually looked up for this answer. Anything that fails the check carries a yellow "unverified" badge.

  2. Your photo, our studio in build

    Send a dim, tilted phone photo and get back an on-brand post or reel. Enhance, never invent: Nisha checks that the building, the dish or the haircut was not altered.

  3. It learns you, visibly

    Every edit becomes a rule linked to the approval it came from, and the share of drafts you approve without edits is tracked week by week on your scorecard.

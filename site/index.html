Your growth plan, in plain words.

Three goals, not a dashboard. Each one says where you are now and where we'll get you: "Get to 360 signups a week."

  • We propose the plan from your website. You tap OK, or change a number. That is the whole setup on your side.
  • Each month your team checks its own progress, adjusts what it works on and writes one short note.
  • A goal reads Going well, Needs a push or Behind, with one line on why.
  • Until your own numbers arrive, a goal says "We'll know after your first full week" rather than guessing.

Nothing goes out without your OK.

When your team wants to do something that costs money, speaks in public or moves a goal, it puts a card in front of you and waits.

  • Each card says what it is, who made it and what saying OK will do.
  • Edit a draft and your team keeps your change as a rule for next time. You can read every rule it has learned.
  • Today, an approved post is saved as a ready draft for you to publish. Nothing posts by itself. Posting straight to Instagram and Google comes later, after those companies approve us, and each post will still wait for your tap.

Prefer chat? Your team is in Discord too.

Ask in your own words, the way you would message a person. It is the same team and the same answers as in the app.

  • Ask

    Write your question in your team's channel. Each question gets its own thread, so one conversation never gets lost in another.

  • Watch

    While the team works, one message updates as each person starts and finishes a step. Then the answer arrives with its sources.

  • Approve

    Waiting cards appear with Approve and Reject buttons, and each business has its own private area that only its owner and Allevia's staff can see.

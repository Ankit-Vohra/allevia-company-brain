The office: who is doing what, right now.

Your team sits on one open floor, with each group on its own rug: Chief of Staff, Center of Excellence, Library, Analytics, Marketing, Content Studio, Tech and Operations. A desk lights up while its person works.

Ask a question and a speech bubble opens over each person who picks it up. The dogs carry documents between desks, and the loop on the right is the real office at its own pace. Point at a dog and it tells you how many documents it has carried today.

The line above the plan says how the business is doing this week, with a comparison ("up 18% on where you started"). Below it, what is waiting for your OK and one next step. Everything else is one tap away.

Waiting for your OK.

One card at a time. Approve, Edit or Reject.

  • The card says what it is, who made it and what happens if you say OK. The full draft and where it goes are one tap behind "Details".
  • If you edit a draft first, your team keeps the edit as a rule and applies it next time. Every rule is listed under "Things your team learned from your edits".
  • Saying OK to a post saves the draft for you. Nothing is posted by Allevia.

Studio: a rough phone photo, made ready.

Send a dim, tilted phone photo. Tara looks at it and marks what must not change. The studio straightens and brightens it, and Nisha checks the result against your original before you see it. in build

What is live, and what is not. The cleaned-up version in the middle is what the Studio makes today, with no AI picture tool involved. The right-hand picture is an AI relight made once, by hand, for our trial and replayed. Live AI edits for customers are not switched on yet. Nothing here is posted anywhere, and when Nisha thinks an AI edit invented something that was not in your photo, it is held back and the app says why.

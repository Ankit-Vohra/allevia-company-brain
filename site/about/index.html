Built in India, for owners who did the hard part already.

Allevia is for people whose business already works: customers, revenue, a reputation. They got from nothing to something on their own craft. Getting from there to the next level takes marketing, numbers, a better website and follow-ups, and they have neither the time nor the know-how. That is the gap we work on.

Who is behind it.

Allevia is built and supported by a small team in India, for owner-run businesses in the United States, the United Kingdom, the UAE and Australia. We set the team up for you, because the real obstacle for most owners is not a lack of AI tools but that nobody sets one up properly and keeps it running.

Allevia was built with two real businesses as its first customers: Aamdani, a Hindi-first jobs platform, and AVS Atelier, an architecture studio. The first version was made during the Graph Hacks hackathon in October 2026, with the company memory built on FalkorDB.

What we believe.

  • An owner should understand any screen in five seconds, with no training. If something needs explaining, it goes behind a "Show how" button.
  • An answer without its sources is a guess.
  • The owner says OK. Nothing speaks in public, spends money or moves a goal without it.
  • We would rather say "we don't know yet" than make a number up.

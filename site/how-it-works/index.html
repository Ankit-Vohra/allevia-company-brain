Three steps. Most of them are ours.

You paste your website. We set your team up. After that, your job is to say OK or No, and to tell your team things in your own words.

1

Paste your website

Give us your website address and say which country you are in. Your team reads your public pages the way a new hire would: what you sell, who it is for, how you look and how you sound.

  • It takes a few minutes, and you watch a short progress screen: your website, your look, Google, your business, your methods, your filing, your team.
  • We read only what is public. We call ourselves AlleviaBot, follow your site's robots.txt, and never log in anywhere.
  • If your site blocks us, we say so in plain words and stop. We do not guess a plan for a business we could not read.
  • An Instagram handle and your Google listing help, and an optional call connects the rest.

2

Meet your team and approve your plan

Your team of __STAFF_WORD__ is briefed on your business. Then it proposes three goals in plain words, each showing where you are now and where we'll get you.

  • Tap OK, or change a number if you want it higher or lower. That is the whole setup on your side.
  • The goals fit your kind of business: a dentist and a software company do not get the same three.
  • Where we cannot yet see a number, the goal says "We'll know where you started after your first full week". We would rather say that than make a number up.

Your team

Every plan has the whole company. Each person has a desk, a job and the tools for it.

3

Your team works, and asks only when it matters

Your team reads your numbers, drafts the posts, checks its own facts and writes Today's note. It asks you first in only three cases:

  • when something costs money,
  • when something speaks in public, in your name,
  • when a change would move a goal.

Everything else it just does, and tells you in Today's note. You say OK or No to what is waiting, and you tell your team things in your own words: "no posts on Sundays", "push the new menu". You see what will change before it does.

Your part, and ours.

What you say, and who takes it.

You write in plain words. Your Chief of Staff, Meera, plans who does what, and the right people pick it up.

Nobody has to write a prompt. If you ask for something Allevia does not do yet, it says so instead of pretending.

For the submission's required video (#14). Recorded after the founder's green flag. Everything is live on

is used and labelled on screen.

"A founder's business lives in their head, their inbox and a dozen tools. Allevia turns it into one company brain, a FalkorDB
graph per business, and an AI team that works from it. Nothing goes out without your OK."

and are labelled as such.

   "Every fact keeps its source and date."
2. **Chat → Your team.** Ask: *Why did online orders drop this week, and what should we do?*

3. **How we got this.** The lit path: online store → customer's email → UPI payments → the error → release 4.12. Say: "That's

# Hackathon submission (draft for the founder's OK)

Graph Hacks: Context for AI Agents (FalkorDB). Deadline 18 October 2026, 23:59 IST.

## Project title

Allevia: the company brain for founders, built on FalkorDB

## Description

A founder's business lives in their head, their inbox and a dozen tools. Allevia turns all of it into one company brain,
a FalkorDB graph per business, and gives the founder an AI team of 13 that works from it. Nothing goes out without the
founder's OK.

**Why a graph:**
- **Every fact keeps its source and its date.** Facts that stop being true are closed, not deleted, so the brain
  remembers what changed and when.
- **Answers follow real links.** "Why did online orders drop 18% this week?" walks from the orders to the checkout
  release, the UPI payment errors and the ad money spent at the same time. The path is shown lit on the map.
- **The brain is efficient.** On our own startup, an agent reading the graph used 8x fewer tokens than stuffing the
  documents into a prompt (75k against 604k), and answered faster.
- **"What breaks if this stops"** is a graph walk: PageRank for what matters, plus the chain behind each dependency.

**What we built:**
- One graph per business, with provenance on every edge.
- 13 agents in departments that plan, check each other and remember.
- A live map of the brain, with "watch it grow".
- Approvals on the web and in Discord.
- Real connections to Gmail, Calendar, Analytics, Search Console and Instagram (through Composio), plus public data from
  Apify.
- Credits, accounts and plans.
- FalkorDB, self-hosted with nightly backups.

**The live demo has three businesses:**
- **Blue Tokai Coffee:** a known brand, with real public data and labelled sample numbers.
- **Aamdani:** our own startup; Allevia grew out of running it.
- **AVS Atelier:** a studio doing well offline and weak online.
